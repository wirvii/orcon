# Orcon brand — approved visual direction

**Status:** The two original concept boards approved by the product owner are the **visual source of truth**. The simplified replacement SVGs formerly committed under brand/logos and brand/mascot were inaccurate and have been deleted.

## Approved reference boards (must be added as original binary files)
1. **Primary board:** original image showing a green orc engineer with black hair tied up, glasses, beard, small tusks, dark navy hoodie, laptop with green-teal geometric mark; left panels with wordmark, app icon, five-color palette, CLI and three facial expressions.
2. **Secondary board:** original image showing orc engineer in multiple poses (friendly, intelligent, reliable, builder, explorer), hero scene with laptop, alternate geometric brand mark, editor, stickers and merchandise.

The product owner re-uploaded these two original boards during the brand review and explicitly rejected the substitute drawings. **Do not redraw or substitute them with simplistic SVGs.** Treat the original boards as source references; production exports should be carefully extracted/recreated from the approved artwork and submitted for visual approval.

## Exact palette shown on primary board
- Bright green: **#22C55E**
- Dark green: **#16A34A**
- Cyan: **#06B6D4**
- Midnight navy: **#0F172A**
- Slate: **#64748B**

This palette takes precedence over earlier speculative palettes. The approved board also uses white/light neutral surfaces. The second board has a different exploratory palette; prefer the primary board as the color reference until a final token review.

## Approved visual characteristics
- Product name: **Orcon**; descriptor **ORCHESTRATION CONTROLLER**.
- Bold rounded modern sans-serif wordmark, white on dark and dark navy on light.
- Symbol: thick geometric hexagonal/loop-like mark with a small open gap, bright green to teal gradient. Preserve original silhouette, angles and proportions; do not substitute a generic outlined hexagon.
- Mascot: muscular yet approachable green orc engineer; pointed ears, modest tusks, dark beard, tied black hair, rectangular black glasses, dark navy hoodie with subtle green branding. Expressive, clever, reliable and professional.
- Art style: polished semi-3D illustrative character, nuanced lighting and texture; **not** flat primitive vector art.
- UI: midnight navy/graphite with green-teal execution highlights; light surfaces for secondary layouts. Accessible, restrained, readable.
- Working tagline: **Build. Orchestrate. Execute. Your way.**

## Required brand assets
- Original approved concept board PNG/JPEG files, tracked in repo.
- Production SVG symbol and wordmark based on the approved design, dark/light variants, icon/favicon, transparent PNG exports.
- High-resolution transparent mascot, headshot, expressions, poses, small-size simplified mascot icon.
- Brand usage guidelines, safe space, minimum sizes, contrast, unacceptable alterations.
- Explicit visual QA: compare exports side-by-side against reference boards, obtain approval before treating assets as final.
