# Orcon Brand Kit — provisional v0.1

Product: **Orcon**, expanded **Orchestration Controller**.
Tagline: **Build. Orchestrate. Execute. Your way.**

## Asset classification
- `logos/orcon-symbol.svg`: provisional editable vector symbol, suitable for design exploration, NOT final approved logo.
- `logos/orcon-lockup-dark.svg`: provisional editable lockup, NOT final lettering.
- Mascot: original friendly orc engineer with guardian-like physical presence. Previously generated PNG concept boards exist in the design discussion, but binary originals are not included in this commit. Production master art, transparency, poses and logo exports are outstanding.

## Visual identity
Graphite/dark navy base, emerald and teal highlights, restrained technical UI. Brand seed colors: #22C55E, #06B6D4, #0F172A, #64748B, #F8FAFC. Other exploratory seed palette in docs/design-system/foundations.md; neither set is final. Typography: Inter or licensed sans-serif equivalent for UI, system monospace for code.

## Use
Primary geometric mark for small sizes and product surfaces; mascot for docs, onboarding, community, stickers and expressive states. Do not imitate a specific fantasy franchise. Do not claim exclusive trademark clearance. Software Apache-2.0 licensing does not automatically define trademark/mascot licensing; publish a separate open asset policy after review.

See docs/design-system/ for component and token specifications.
